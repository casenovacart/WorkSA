Protect yourself from job scams

WorkSA links to external services; a listing appearing on another platform is not a guarantee that it is legitimate. Take a moment to check before you apply.

  • Never pay a recruiter or employer to secure a job, interview or offer.
  • Verify the company, recruiter email address and vacancy on the employer’s official website.
  • Be cautious of guaranteed jobs, unusually high pay for little work, or pressure to act immediately.
  • Do not send banking passwords, PINs or one-time passwords to anyone.

🛡️ Stay alert to job scams

Never pay to secure a job. Verify the employer and vacancy using the company’s official website, and never share banking PINs or one-time passwords.

Read safety checklist ↓

Apply with confidence: a quick safety checklist

  • Never pay a recruiter or employer for an interview, job offer, training or placement.
  • Check that the recruiter’s email domain matches the organisation and verify the vacancy on its official website.
  • Be wary of guaranteed jobs, unusually high pay for little work, requests for urgent payment or pressure to act immediately.
  • Do not share banking passwords, PINs or one-time passwords. Share identity documents only through verified, secure employer channels when genuinely required.
  • WorkSA is an independent guide. A vacancy appearing on an external platform is not a guarantee that it is legitimate.
